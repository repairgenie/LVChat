Notifications
Notifications appear in your operating system. They can also be turned off per channel or per person in your web profile.

Not seeing notifications? Open Windows notification settings and make sure notifications are enabled for “LVChat Messenger” (and that Focus assist isn’t on). The app must be installed via the Setup installer for notifications to appear.

Sounds
Sounds play for new messages while the chat is in the background. Muting a person in your web profile silences their messages too.
